Get To Know Me

About Me

I'm Shadman, a passionate 3D Character Designer and CGI Artist based in India. I breathe life into digital characters — from initial concept sketches to fully game-ready, production-quality assets.

I am a passionate and dedicated 3D Character Artist with a strong interest in creating high-quality game-ready characters. I enjoy every stage of the character creation pipeline, from sculpting and clothing to retopology, UV mapping, texturing, and rendering. I continuously improve my skills by studying anatomy, fabric, and hard-surface details while working on personal projects inspired by AAA games. As a fresher, I am eager to learn from experienced artists, adapt to production workflows, and contribute to creating immersive characters for games..
